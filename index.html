01 About

Building with intention.

I believe the best websites are invisible — they disappear into the brand, letting identity do the talking. For three years I've been building exactly that: websites and digital systems that don't just represent a brand, but become part of it.

My work lives at the intersection of engineering discipline and brand craft. In my full-time role, I contribute to enterprise-grade software systems serving thousands of users. After hours, I run an independent practice building websites and automation for clinics and local businesses — starting with a full local-SEO marketing site for a multi-doctor dental practice in Kerala, and taking on new work from there.

If you are hiring someone to build the digital face of your brand — to read the brief, design with intention, code with care, and treat your identity as their own — we should talk.
